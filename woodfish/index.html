心經日課 · Heart Sutra Daily

開始讀經

從「今日讀經」逐段讀玄奘譯《般若波羅蜜多心經》古典漢文。隨時暫停，下次開啟可回到同一段。在最後一段點「完成讀經」，可在「歷程」查看這一課。

木魚伴讀

閱讀時點右上角波形按鈕開啟伴讀，再輕敲閱讀區。設定中可分別開關敲擊聲與觸覺回饋，並選擇音色。若有觸感但無聲，請檢查 iPhone 是否開了靜音。翻段請使用底部按鈕。

Read one passage at a time

Open Today’s reading to read the original Classical Chinese Heart Sutra as translated by Xuanzang. Pause at any passage and return to it later. Tap Finish reading at the last passage to save a History entry.

Woodfish accompaniment

Tap the waveform during reading, then tap the reading area. Sound, tone and haptics can be set independently. Check your iPhone’s silent setting if you can feel the taps but can’t hear them. Use the bottom buttons to turn passages.
